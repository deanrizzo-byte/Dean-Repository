What We Look For in Top Breeders

AKC Titles & Awards

Champions, Grand Champions, and performance titles demonstrate breeding quality dogs that meet breed standards.

Health Testing (CHIC)

CHIC-certified dogs have completed all recommended health tests for Rhodesian Ridgebacks including hips, elbows, thyroid, and more.

Breeder of Merit

AKC Breeder of Merit designation recognizes breeders who demonstrate a commitment to health, education, and breed preservation.

RRCUS Membership

Members of the Rhodesian Ridgeback Club of the United States agree to abide by a code of ethics for responsible breeding.
